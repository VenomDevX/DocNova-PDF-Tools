Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard 256-bit encryption for data in transit and at rest. Pro and Enterprise plans offer HIPAA compliance mechanisms suitable for healthcare providers.

Do you use my data to train your models?

No. We maintain strict data privacy. Your documents are processed securely and are never used to train our base AI models without explicit, separate consent for custom Enterprise solutions.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Prorated charges will be applied automatically if you upgrade mid-billing cycle.

What happens after my 14-day trial?

If you don't choose a paid plan before your trial ends, your account will automatically be downgraded to the Free tier, ensuring you never lose access to your existing documents.
